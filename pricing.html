GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

If your question isn’t covered, send us a message and tell us what you need.

How long does a website take?

A small website may take around 2 days to 1 week, while a larger or custom project may take several weeks. Timing depends on scope, feedback and content readiness.

What do you need to provide a quote?

A short description of your goals, desired pages or features, any reference websites, your preferred timeline and an approximate budget are a good start.

Are hosting and domain names included?

Not by default. We can help arrange them, but registration, hosting, premium tools and other third-party charges are identified separately in your proposal.

Can you maintain a website you didn’t build?

Often, yes. We first review the platform, access and current condition, then confirm what support is feasible and quote accordingly.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. We can improve technical and on-page SEO foundations, but search rankings depend on many factors and cannot responsibly be guaranteed.
